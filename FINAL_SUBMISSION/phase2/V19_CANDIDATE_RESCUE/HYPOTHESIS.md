# Phase V19: Hypotheses

## Hypothesis H1: Redundant Periodic Slots
*Standard greedy NMS captures dozens of redundant replica peaks from a single dominant periodic family located on peripheral boundaries, starving the queue of other physical cell locations.*

## Hypothesis H2: Representative Diversity Recovery
*Extracting $K=200$ raw correlation peaks and capping each periodic family at $\le 2\text{ representatives}$ (selected by maximum center-weighted score) will liberate slots to recover suppressed Ground Truth candidates without expanding downstream pool size beyond 50.*
